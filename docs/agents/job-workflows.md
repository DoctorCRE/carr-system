Job types, stages, required skills, and acceptance evidence are defined in [ops/config/job-workflows.v1.yaml](../../ops/config/job-workflows.v1.yaml).

Job briefs require `Title:` and one-sentence `Description:` lines starting with imperatives from [job-card-verbs.v1.json](../../mcp-server/src/job-card-verbs.v1.json); use two or three title words with the distinguishing words first, following [the readable text principles](https://fsdatalab.github.io/blog/agent-text-at-scale/).
