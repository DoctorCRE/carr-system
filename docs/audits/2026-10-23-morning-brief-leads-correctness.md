**Consequence/reproduction.** A synthetic response with one ready Needs Joe item and unavailable renewals/costs produced `groups: []` and `unavailable: []`. The UI therefore takes its “No to-dos” branch despite a waiting decision and failed sources. This is a confirmed product-contract gap even though the narrow card layout is deliberate.

Define one shared list of required sections; propagate partial/unavailable states into visible coverage and speech. Reserve “No to-dos” for the explicitly named scope, and distinguish “no selected priority cards” from no work. **1–2 days**, slice A.
