How to read this board

Heat score weighs four things: how close the real-estate decision is (the biggest factor), financial strength and production history, the timing window before someone signs, and fit with the client profile. Scores move as facts change.

Stage guess is exactly that, a read from public records stated so it can be corrected on the first call. Vocabulary: pre-space → entity formed → expanding in place → relocating → renewal window.

Source sensitivity is graded on every lead. STANDARD = business public records. PERSONAL = the signal derives from someone's personal life or an unannounced private move (home purchase, pre-announcement departure, household signals). Personal-tagged leads follow the handling rules: the detection is NEVER the opener, outreach references only what they have publicly self-announced, warmer channel and slower clock, and every touch passes the "how did you know to call me?" test. Hardship-derived signals get a service posture only, no urgency framing ever.

The play is the move to make; Winning it is the deeper strategy: the person's real situation and psychology, the natural channel, what not to reveal knowing, how fast the lead decays, and the realistic risk.

Sources are cited on every lead in the registry's Source Detail. Nothing on this board is contacted without your per-lead go. The full books: Lead registry (every L-row) and Client roster (every C-row).

Day zero · the entity filing

The primary detector. Filing a business entity is the act of intent: a person committing to a practice, and a practice needs a building. In Florida the entity always precedes the org NPI by weeks or months (the Pellegrino lesson: 97 days late on a lead sitting in a free daily file), so Sunbiz leads and NPPES confirms. This lane also catches the two classes the 9,300-lead reservoir structurally misses: relocators and all of Alabama. Vets, who never appear in NPI, arrive here too.

The address workup

Five questions, in order: who owns the building and since when · is it on the market, and how old is the listing · who else is in it and are they leaving · who represents the landlord (do we know them) · is there a build-out permit. An address is a lead to dig into, and the digging keeps going until it changes the action or stops paying.

0 to 10: lease-event proximity (a pre-space entity scores the full 4), formation strength, vertical + territory fit, warm network path. 6+ goes TOP, 3 to 5 WATCH, below 3 logged. Active clients are suppressed. Every score carries its evidence one-liner.

The lease clock · people already practicing here

Everything above finds people on their way in. This lane works the people already in the territory, on a different clock: the lease. Joe's reframe governs it end to end: the building points at a person, and the person is the lead. A lease coming due means the doctor is approaching a real-estate decision of some kind: renew, relocate and lease, relocate and buy, buy the space they occupy, build, sale-leaseback, or exit. The job is to be in the room before they decide.

The reservoir · 9,300+ routed practitioners

The standing base layer: every licensed territory practitioner, routed into a segment with its own play and its own clock. Built by joining the FL DOH licensure file (identity, license age, email), the Sunbiz owner index (who owns an entity), the tax rolls (who owns a building), and NPPES. Alabama runs the inverted stack: NPPES supplies the addresses the AL board withholds, the board roster supplies the license clock, and there are no emails, so Alabama is phone-first and more manual by nature.

The associate window · the longest clock

An associate owns nothing, works at somebody else's practice, and is the future practice owner. License age 3 to 15 years marks the going-independent window (Brown went at 7, Pellegrino at 14). By the time an entity files, the engine is already late; this lane means we knew them for years first.
