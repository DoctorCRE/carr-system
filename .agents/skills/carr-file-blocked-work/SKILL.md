### 647f843d

THE DEFERRAL GATE: A LOOP IS FOR WORK THAT IS GENUINELY BLOCKED AND AT RISK OF BEING LOST, AND FOR NOTHING ELSE. Before opening any open_loop, the session must name why THIS session cannot do the work now, from a closed list of states of the world OUTSIDE the session: human_only (needs Joe or Dell in person — a call, a signature, a site visit, a login only he holds) · counterparty (waiting on a named landlord, broker, client or vendor) · ruling (needs Joe's decision, and the question is stated in one line) · external_event (a dated event must arrive first, and the date is named) · other_lane (another lane's in-flight deliverable, named) · capability (a credential, gate or verb this session cannot hold, named — rule 1b8e7f43's case). There is deliberately no value meaning "later". A session that cannot name one of these has just demonstrated it can do the work, and the answer is to do it and file nothing.

THIS EXTENDS 179be4b8; IT DOES NOT REPLACE IT, AND BOTH ARE ACTIVE ON PURPOSE. 179be4b8 said the same thing in Joe's words on 2026-08-08 — "why would you put them off? thats the exact reason we have a giant growing list of loops. you constantly opt to not finish the job" — and it was ACTIVE and recited. On 2026-08-09 the list stood at 189 open rows and Joe asked for it again. The failure was not disobedience: 179be4b8 binds at BUILD WRAP-UP, on follow-ups and known limits noticed during a build, while most loops are opened mid-session by a session that simply decided not to do the thing. Nothing bound at the moment of deferral. THE TWO COVER DIFFERENT MOMENTS AND THE PAIR IS LOAD-BEARING: this rule intercepts the FILING, and 179be4b8 intercepts the WRAP-UP — including the case where a session names a "known limit" in prose and files nothing at all, where there is no add-loop call for this gate to catch. Gating the filing creates a fresh incentive to skip filing and mention it in chat, which is exactly what rule 1b8e7f43 forbids ("Report AND file; reporting alone is the failure this rule closes, because chat is not a record"). The three together close the triangle: 1b8e7f43 says a blocked action must be FILED, this rule says a filing must name a REAL blocker, and 179be4b8 says test at wrap-up whether you could just build it. Retiring either of the other two opens a hole this one cannot cover.

SO THE RULE IS ENFORCED IN CODE, NOT REMEMBERED. add-loop refuses an open_loop without `blocker` and a specific `blocker_detail`, and refuses detail text that names a feeling about time ("later", "revisit", "when there's time", "tbd", "low priority") instead of a person, ruling, date or credential. Migration 0081 carries the same closed list as a check constraint, so the record cannot hold what the verb would not accept. team_loop and action_required are not gated — the blocker is the other partner by construction — and idea rows are parked by design. v_loop_no_blocker names every row carrying no blocker, and its bound action is DO IT OR CLOSE IT, never re-file it; its predates_gate flag is drawn at the deploy instant (2026-08-09 22:20:28Z), so a FALSE row means the gate was bypassed and is a defect to investigate.

THE COROLLARY THAT MATTERS MOST: a session holding the context is the cheapest builder that item will ever get. Every parked-but-buildable item becomes a row a future session pays full context-loading cost to pick up, which is precisely how the list grew to 189.
