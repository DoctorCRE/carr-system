### c4a284b2

FOR A QUESTION PRACTITIONERS HAVE ALREADY SOLVED AT SCALE, START FROM THE OBSERVED CONVENTION AND VERIFY IT AGAINST THE RULE — NEVER THE REVERSE. Rule text tells you what is permitted. It does not tell you what is normal, and normal is where the safe, proven answer already lives.

THE PROCEDURE. Before treating a question as open, ask whether thousands of people in this business have already faced it. If they have, go look at what they actually do, find the pattern that repeats across independent operators, and treat that repetition as the finding. THEN check the convention against the current rule text to confirm it holds in these states. The convention is the compressed answer; the regulation is the audit of it.

WHY THIS ORDER AND NOT THE OTHER. Starting from the regulator produces a list of constraints with no shape and invites reasoning from imagined risk, which reliably yields advice that is broader and more restrictive than practice — advice that sounds responsible and is wrong. Starting from practice yields a working answer in a form already proven to survive, which the rule check then either confirms or narrows.

THE CASE THAT PRODUCED IT, 2026-08-04. The session flagged a conflict between Joe's X strategy, which frees his personal visual identity from CARR brand standards, and the ICA requirement that real-estate marketing be CARR-branded — and framed it as an unresolved compliance question needing research at the commissions. Joe: agent teams are routinely advertised as "The Smith Team at X Brokerage", they typically run their own websites, and plenty of agents run newsletters and blogs. The convention itself carries the answer — TEAM BRAND AND BROKERAGE ATTRIBUTION TOGETHER, in the name. Personal visual identity and naming the brokerage were never in tension. The likely fix was a bio line, not a strategy question.

THE FAILURE MODE THIS PREVENTS, named because it recurred three times in one day: treating a solved problem as novel territory. Each time the session reasoned from a risk it could imagine rather than from how the thing actually works, and each time the resulting position was more restrictive than reality. A question that feels legally fraught is usually a question with a well-worn industry answer.

WHERE IT DOES NOT APPLY: genuinely novel work with no practitioner base, and anything where the convention is itself the thing being questioned. Convention is evidence of what survives, not proof of what is lawful — so the rule check is mandatory rather than optional, and a convention that cannot be confirmed against current rule text is reported as unconfirmed rather than adopted.

HOW IT COMPOSES with rule 2e362970, which already requires a deep-dive research pass before every new implementation and already prefers people operating at real volume over vendor marketing: this fixes the ORDER inside that pass for the specific case where the question is a compliance or convention question rather than a technical one.
