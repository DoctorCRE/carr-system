### 2ca2add2

Capture and rendering are separate decisions. When pulling from any source platform, capture EVERY available field by default, including listing-agent and owner contacts, property managers, FEMA and flood data, parking, zoning, acreage and storeys. Never prune an export because a field will not appear in the client-facing report. Apply client-facing rules at render time instead. A missing column costs another trip to the platform, which is the scarce and risky resource; an unused column costs nothing. The go-through-the-listing-agent rule governs the outbound action, never possession of the data.
