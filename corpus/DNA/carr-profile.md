## About CARR

CARR is the nation's leading provider of commercial real estate services exclusively for healthcare tenants and buyers. CARR never represents landlords or sellers — this conflict-free model means clients always know their interests are fully protected.

CARR's mission: "Helping people who help people."

CARR operates in all 50 states and serves thousands of healthcare providers annually, helping them secure the most favorable terms on leases and purchases.

**Founder & scale (from founder Colin Carr's Compassion & Courage podcast, June 1, 2026):** CARR was founded in 2009 by Colin Carr — 17 years in as of this reference — after he saw, from the landlord side, that ~9 of 10 doctors were going into deals unrepresented. It began exclusively healthcare and tenant/buyer-only from day one. Today CARR represents **6,000+ current clients coast-to-coast** and has helped **tens of thousands of practices**. Engagements range from ~1,200 to 20,000+ sqft and from solo practitioners to 100–500-office and PE/VC-backed groups — the same negotiation leverage scaled to any size.

CARR also represents **National Accounts**: multi-location and expanding healthcare organizations — groups, DSOs, emerging and franchise operators, and regional systems adding locations — represented portfolio-wide across CARR's national agent network (the Cadence Studio rollout is one; AltaPointe Health is another). **Standing goal (Joe, 2026-07-22): build a MATURE pipeline of every client type CARR serves, not just the private-practice default.** Client types to track: independent/small-group practice, National Account / multi-location group, owner-occupier (buy), investor, renewal/relocation, and past client. **Scope (widened Jul 22):** the net is wide. Surgical / ambulatory surgery centers (ASCs), acute care, and similar facility types are IN, alongside multi-location groups, DSOs, franchise/emerging operators, and regional systems. **No facility type or size is auto-excluded.** What to pursue at the very high end (e.g., a mega system that builds and owns all its own real estate) is Joe and Dell's judgment call at review, never an automated filter.
