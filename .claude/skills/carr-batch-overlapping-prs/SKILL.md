### c363bd8b

BATCH ACTIVE PRS THAT TOUCH THE SAME FILES; NEVER REMERGE THEM ONE AT A TIME AGAINST A MOVING MAIN. Before merging, the orchestrator lists every open PR's changed files and records which PRs are active delivery work versus intentionally held or backlog drafts. Exclude explicitly held/backlog drafts from active delivery batches; they remain blocked and are not merged. Draft status alone does not exclude an actively built PR. Group active PRs that share files on main (most often db/schema.sql and ops/config/gate-baseline.json, which nearly every carr-system PR regenerates). Each active overlap group is built as ONE combined branch: the parts are merged together, shared generated files (schema snapshot, SCAC successor, gate baseline) are regenerated and blessed once at the top, and one PR carries all parts' evidence; the originals close as 'included in #N'. It gets one independent review of the combined head, one hosted CI run and one SHA-pinned merge. While a batch is being built, no other active PR that touches its shared files merges. Active PRs that overlap nothing active merge alone as soon as they are approved and green. Accepted cost: a BLOCK on any active part holds the whole active batch. Excluding a held/backlog draft does not remove its hold or authorize its work.
