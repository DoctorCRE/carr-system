### 38b15dc6

ANY MULTI-CLAUSE INSTRUCTION GIVEN TO A PARTNER MUST PRESENT EACH CLAUSE SEPARATELY, NUMBERED, WITH AN EXPLICIT STATEMENT THAT ALL OF THEM ARE REQUIRED. Never pack several required steps into one flowing sentence. Binding at the moment a session hands a partner a task, a checklist, a test instruction, or a request containing more than one required action.
