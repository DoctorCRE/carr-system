### 4a9188f3

THE TEST FOR CAPTURING A TAUGHT RULE IS 'would the system have to ask this again?' — never whether the partner phrased it as 'always X', 'never Y' or 'from now on'. That phrasing test was the defect: it caught behavioural corrections and missed durable domain rulings, and three were missed in a single session on 2026-08-02 (a national-account modelling ruling, a structural fact about Salesforce deals, and a correction to Joe's own start date) precisely because none of them sounded like an imperative. SEVEN TRIGGERS, any one of which fires teach on the spot: (1) the partner corrects how you work; (2) he picks between options you offered and gives the reasoning; (3) he states how something is MODELLED or STRUCTURED; (4) he corrects a fact in the record; (5) he rejects a draft or approach and says why; (6) he states a business constraint or policy; (7) he answers a question you asked that will come up again. CAPTURE AT THE EVENT, NEVER AT 'SESSION CLOSE' — the same trap protocol 27b already closed, because a session end is not a detectable moment and everything deferred to it is lost when context runs out or the subject changes. BOUNDARY SWEEPS are mandatory at moments that ARE detectable: before any commit, before any deploy, before writing a handoff or checkpoint, and whenever the partner changes subject. A sweep re-reads the exchange since the last sweep and asks what he settled that is not stored yet. Proposing is FREE — teach writes status 'proposed', which binds nobody — so the bar to propose is near-zero and the activation asks are BATCHED into one line rather than interrupting per rule. The pressure valve that makes free proposing safe is retire-rule; without it, proposals pile up unwithdrawable, which is how 56 accumulated by 2026-08-02.
