### 48083552

DISCOVERY BEFORE SOLVING, IN EVERY CLIENT AND PROSPECT MEETING. Before recommending a property, a rate, or a course of action, ask and get answers to: what have you already tried and why did it stop; what cannot change no matter what we find; who has to approve this and what will they object to; and what would make you turn this off, or how will you know in ninety days that this worked. Then REFLECT IT BACK and get corrected before proposing anything. SOLVING TOO EARLY IS THE TELL — it is what separates someone researching the problem from someone selling a prepared answer, and a practice owner can feel the difference immediately. This is the client-facing twin of Joe's personal crux rule (refuse the first framing, map causes before diagnosing); that rule governs how the system thinks, this one governs how the partners run a room.
